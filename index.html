Questions founders ask

What are the terms?

A fixed fee of $2,000, agreed upfront. The plan is always yours. Continue within 30 days and the fee is credited to your first month. The retainer is scoped to your plan before you agree to it.

What does the sprint take from us?

Two live sessions of 1 to 2 hours, and one piece of buyer evidence, such as 2 or 3 sales call recordings.

Other agencies offer a free audit.

An audit tells you what's wrong. The sprint gives you the message, the segments, 2 or 3 chosen channels, and week-one actions with owners. If a review is all you need, we're not the right fit.

Shouldn't we just hire a marketer?

For most companies, that's the right long-term answer. A first hire is often junior and needs months to learn the science. We can run it now, and the plan gives whoever you hire a starting point.

Nobody here has time to run a 90-day plan.

That's true for most companies at your stage. The plan starts with steps you can take alone in week one, and the retainer exists so someone runs the rest.

Is our technology too specialized for outsiders?

We've launched direct air capture, carbon removal, EV charging, solar and heat pump products. You'll see your story at the founder check-in, before we choose any tactics.

Are two weeks enough to understand our market?

Partly. Where buyer evidence is thin, the sprint labels its conclusions as hypotheses to test. The retainer tests and updates them every 3 months.

What results can you guarantee?

None before the plan exists. KPIs are agreed in your plan and reported monthly.
